Story of the Bakery

North Star Bakery began as a small pop-up bakery in downtown Topeka which grew into a full size bakery where everything is still fresh every morning, by hand.

Sourcing Philosophy

We buy simple local ingredients in Topeka and Kansas and keep our recipes free from artifical preservatives and flavorings.
